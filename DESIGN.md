# Design System

## Theme

A personal visual diary assembled from travel photographs, handwritten chapter titles, soft blue paper, pinned notes, and fragments of a digital scrapbook. The mood is airy, affectionate, slightly strange, and visibly handmade.

## Color

- Deep ink blue: `#082f66`
- Ocean blue: `#0877b8`
- Sky blue: `#48cae4`
- Mist blue: `#caf0f8`
- Pale paper blue: `#eaf8fb`
- Warm paper: `#fffaf1`
- Handwritten accent: `#f25f5c`

The original blue family remains dominant. Warm paper and coral appear as small human counterpoints.

## Typography

- Handwritten headings and wordmark: Pacifico.
- Body text: Alegreya Medium upright, chosen for open counters, a humanist rhythm, and comfortable long-form reading.
- Italic Alegreya is reserved for occasional letter-like emphasis rather than entire paragraphs.
- Navigation, controls, and small labels use Alegreya upright for clarity.
- Pacifico is restricted to short phrases, ideally under six words.
- Long titles use Alegreya or are split into a short handwritten title plus a readable subtitle.

## Layout

- Use full-bleed photography as atmosphere and narrative.
- Compose sections as scrapbook spreads with overlaps, tape-like details, captions, and asymmetry.
- Vary section height and density. Avoid repeating the same grid.
- Keep content readable with narrow text measures and calm vertical rhythm.
- On mobile, remove overlaps that compromise reading but preserve image-led personality.
- Large paper and image surfaces use a restrained 6–10px radius, like gently softened paper edges rather than pill-shaped cards.

## Components

- Header: small handwritten wordmark with quiet text navigation; on mobile, navigation opens as a compact warm-paper hanging sheet rather than a full-height dark drawer.
- Hero: original snow landscape as a full-screen visual field with a compact personal introduction.
- Chapter title: Pacifico phrase paired with a small uppercase label.
- Pinned note: warm paper, subtle rotation, handwritten title, readable body copy.
- Project story: large artifact images with annotations and a conversational narrative.
- Gallery: irregular photo journal with captions that remain accessible on touch devices.
- Contact: original background image with a simple paper-like form.

## Motion

- Use gentle opacity and small translate reveals only where they support the feeling of pages settling into place.
- The Anima Isle chapter control is a small sea route: chapter markers act as buoys and a sailboat travels between them as the story advances.
- Never use dramatic corporate entrance choreography.
- Disable non-essential motion when reduced motion is requested.

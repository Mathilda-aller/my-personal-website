A note from me

About me.

Thank you for wanting to know me a little better. I study French at Beijing Foreign Studies University, and I have also been teaching myself how to build with data, code, and AI.

I am interested in the point where precision meets feeling: language that carries a whole culture, technology that begins with a human need, and strength that does not require becoming hard.

Language

French taught me to notice the tiny distance between what someone says and what they mean. I carry that attentiveness into everything else.

Building

I love the moment when a vague idea becomes something real enough to click, test, break, repair, and finally share with another person.

Movement

Strength training, hiking, and long walks bring me back into my body. They remind me that patience is still a form of motion.

People

I treasure sincere connections. A good conversation, a shared meal, or simply being fully present with someone can change the shape of a day.
